What this demonstration is testing

Non-limited allocation

Can reliable short-term forecasts reduce the number of irrigations and total ML/ha while maintaining crop water status and yield?

Limited allocation

Can improved use of effective rainfall preserve scarce irrigation water for later crop demand and reduce water-stress-related yield loss?

Parameters to explore with CoP farmers

Rain amount × probability, forecast horizon, acceptable delay, SWD trigger and maximum irrigation amount.
